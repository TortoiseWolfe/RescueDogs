Petfinder still owns **Start Your Inquiry**, **See How You Match**, and
**Become a Sponsor** — leave those alone. For **pilot dogs only**, add this

> You’ll create an account, submit once, and see where your application stands.

**Where to get the Apply URL:** After you save a pet in Raised Paws, open
**Edit** — the browser URL contains the pet id. Or ask your Raised Paws contact.

### Parallel workflow (pilot — don’t change everything at once)

| How someone reaches you                                | What you do                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Raised Paws link** in the Petfinder bio (pilot dogs) | Review in **Applications** on your phone — this is the queue for that pilot dog |
| **Petfinder “Start Your Inquiry”** (built-in form)     | Handle as you do today — still a valid lead                                     |
| **Petfinder “See How You Match”**                      | Their pre-screen; unchanged                                                     |
| **Email / call** from the Petfinder listing            | Handle as you do today (Word/PDF attachment if that’s your current habit)       |
| **Other dogs** not in the pilot                        | Your normal process unchanged                                                   |

For **pilot dogs**, we want the Raised Paws link to be the clear **full
application** path. You keep email folders and paper files if you want — we’re
fixing the front door and “where am I?” follow-ups, not replacing Petfinder or
your whole filing system yet.

---

### When someone applies through your Raised Paws link

1. Applicant creates a Raised Paws account (email + password) and fills the form.
2. They see a **live status tracker** immediately.
3. You get an **email** at your rescue contact address (and the row appears under **Applications**).
4. Open **Applications** → tap the row → read answers and their email.
5. Move status when something real happens (even small updates help adopters).
6. You can **Message** in the app or email them from the address on the detail screen.

---

### Status meanings (plain language)

| Status              | What adopters see         | What it means for you                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Submitted**       | Application received      | New app — in your queue                |
| **Under Review**    | Team is reviewing         | You’re reading / first screen          |
| **Reference Check** | Checking references       | Your reference step                    |
| **Home Visit**      | Home visit step           | Foster / home visit scheduled or done  |
| **Approved**        | Approved                  | Ready for meet & greet / adoption path |
| **Not Selected**    | Not selected for this pet | Next in line — they’re not ghosted     |

### Pet info to upload (per dog)

Match what you already put on Petfinder when you can:

- Photos (required for listings)
- Name, age, breed, size / weight if you have it
- Medical flags you already publish (spay/neuter, vaccines, special needs)
- Kid / dog / cat compatibility notes
- Adoption fee (you can put it in **notes** for now)
- Story: personality hook, background, hard requirements (e.g. cat-free home)
