### Parallel workflow (pilot — don't change everything at once)

| How someone applies                                    | What you do                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Petfinder link** to Raised Paws (pilot dogs)         | Review in **Applications** on your phone — this is the queue for that dog |
| **Petfinder inquiry** or email with Word/PDF (old way) | Handle exactly as you do today                                            |
| **Other dogs** not in the pilot                        | Your normal process unchanged                                             |

For **pilot dogs only**, the Raised Paws link is the primary apply path. You
keep email folders and paper files if you want — we're fixing the front door and
"where am I?" follow-ups, not replacing your whole filing system yet.

---

### When someone applies through your link

1. Applicant creates a Raised Paws account (email + password) and fills the form.
2. They see a **live status tracker** immediately.
3. You open **Applications** → tap the row → read answers and their email.
4. Move status when something real happens (even small updates help adopters).
5. You can **Message** in the app or email them from the address on the detail screen.

**Today:** check **Applications** on your phone — email alerts when a new app
arrives are coming soon.

---

### Status meanings (plain language)

| Status              | What adopters see         | What it means for you                  |
| ------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Submitted**       | Application received      | New app — in your queue                |
| **Under Review**    | Team is reviewing         | You're reading / first screen          |
| **Reference Check** | Checking references       | Your reference step                    |
| **Home Visit**      | Home visit step           | Foster / home visit scheduled or done  |
| **Approved**        | Approved                  | Ready for meet & greet / adoption path |
| **Not Selected**    | Not selected for this pet | Next in line — they're not ghosted     |
