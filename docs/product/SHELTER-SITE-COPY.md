# Shelter site copy — Apply / Track blurbs

**Audience:** Pilot shelters pasting links on their website, Petfinder, social, or desk QR.  
**Goal:** The handoff feels like _their_ adoption portal — not “go to another pet website.”

Replace `[Shelter Name]`, `[Apply URL]` (e.g. `https://raisedpaws.com/adopt`), and `[Track URL]` (e.g. `https://raisedpaws.com/applications`).

Aligns with [VISION.md](./VISION.md) (application portal framing) and [PILOT-AGREEMENT.md](./PILOT-AGREEMENT.md).

---

## Website — pet page / Adopt button

**Button label:** Apply to adopt with [Shelter Name]

**Short blurb under the button:**

> Ready to welcome this pet home? Start your adoption application with **[Shelter Name]**. You’ll get a live status tracker so you always know where things stand — no wondering if your application got lost.

**One-line footer (optional):**

> Application and status tracking powered by Raised Paws for [Shelter Name].

## Website — How to adopt page

> **How adoption works at [Shelter Name]**
>

> 2. Submit your application through our adoption portal.
> 3. Watch your application status update as our team reviews it.
>
> [**Start your application**]([Apply URL])  
> Already applied? [**Check your status**]([Track URL])

## Petfinder / Adopt-a-Pet bio

> To apply for this pet with [Shelter Name], use our adoption portal: [Apply URL] — you’ll get live updates on your application status.

### Where to paste (Petfinder-first rescues)

Many listings already ship Petfinder-native buttons (**Start Your Inquiry**, **See How You Match**, **Become a Sponsor**). Those stay. Put the Raised Paws blurb in the **story / description** for pilot pets — ideally right after the personality hook — so adopters who want a full application with status tracking have a clear path without fighting Petfinder’s UI.

Do **not** tell the partner to remove Inquiry during the pilot (Pattern B: parallel). For pilot dogs, treat the Raised Paws link as the preferred full-application path when followed.

Longer partner-facing wording: [FIRST-PARTNER-ZOOM-HANDOUT.md](./FIRST-PARTNER-ZOOM-HANDOUT.md).

## Pattern C (Raised Paws + required shelter packet)

> Start with [Shelter Name]’s application portal so we can track your status: [Apply URL]  
> After you submit, you’ll also complete our required adoption packet. Completing both keeps your application moving.

---

## What not to put on their site

- “Visit Raised Paws to browse pets” (makes Raised Paws the destination)
- A bare `raisedpaws.com` with no shelter name in the sentence
- Their old Google Form as the **primary** Apply button for pilot pets (breaks anti-ghosting)

---

## Staff one-liner (“why another site?”)

> It’s our application and status portal — same idea as an online form, except applicants can see progress instead of emailing us for updates.

**Co-brand line:** _[Shelter Name] adoption portal, powered by Raised Paws_
